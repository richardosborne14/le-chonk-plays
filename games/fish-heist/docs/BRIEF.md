# Brief

## What this app is

A small single-player turn-based heist game: move Le Chonk the cat across a 7x7 board of crates to steal the fish in the top-right corner while a guard dog hunts you one step at a time.

## Who uses it

One person playing on a laptop or a phone, in short rounds.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Sound or animation
- Difficulty levels or anything beyond the guard dog
- Saving a round in progress across reloads (only the tally persists)
- Changing the player name mid-round

_Agreed in the initial scoping conversation; the full record, including what was considered and rejected, is in `docs/decisions/000-initial-scope.md`._
